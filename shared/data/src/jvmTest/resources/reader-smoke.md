# The Persistent Reader

This is a real imported Markdown document used to verify that Narratify keeps
the book and reading position after the reader closes.

## A second section

The quick brown fox jumps over the lazy dog. This paragraph is deliberately
long enough to make the reading view scroll on a phone-sized display.

One morning, a reader opened a local file without signing in or connecting to
the network. The file remained inside managed app storage, while its identity
and resilient text quotation were recorded in SQLite.

The reader returned later and found the same words waiting at the top of the
screen. Layout could change, but the exact text, prefix, and suffix still made
the location recoverable.

End of the smoke-test document.
